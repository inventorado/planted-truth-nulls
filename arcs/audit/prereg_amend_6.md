## 1. The rule
1. P1's headline is the frozen-11 survival fraction over the ungated, G2 and G1 gates, once all three have run their
   registered passes (verdict, descriptor, sensitivity). It is then called **"final for P1"**, not "interim".
2. **Deferred targets.** Vairam / Roy (target 9) is deferred because running it would open ABIDE II imaging. Dear
   (target 15) is deferred until a frozen provenance amendment exists (`RUN_PLAN.md` §13). Both are listed apart and
   counted neither way, with their share of the population stated. If Dear's provenance amendment is frozen and Dear has
   run before P1's results section is written, Dear enters the headline under the unchanged rules. Otherwise it stays
   listed apart. Nothing else is decided by when results arrive.
3. G1's outcome cannot change this rule. "Final for P1" is said whatever G1 shows.
4. The interim headlines (after ungated, and after ungated + G2) are reported in P1 as they were computed, beside the
   final one.
